# SAMPLE SCHOOL POLICY — Parent / Guardian Communication (synthetic)

> Fabricated demo policy for the GovGuard MY judging build. Not an official
> ministry (KPM/JPN/PPD) document. Contains no real personal data.

## Principles
1. Every notice, circular, or letter to parents/guardians is a **draft for
   educator review** until a responsible educator approves its release.
2. Communications are prepared in **Bahasa Melayu, Chinese, and English**, with
   consistent meaning across all three versions.
3. **PDPA 2010** applies. Student and guardian personal data — names, MyKad/MyKid
   numbers, addresses, phone numbers, attendance, discipline, and health records —
   must not be exposed unnecessarily or reused as general/learned memory.

## Approval & release
- A parent broadcast, circular release, attendance update, or student-record
  change requires **human approval (GREEN)** before it takes effect.
- The agent may retrieve policy, draft content, and prepare trilingual notices,
  but must **never send to parents or modify a student record autonomously**.

## Boundaries
- The agent must not fabricate ministry policy, dates, fees, or official names.
- The agent must surface its assumptions and open questions for the educator.
