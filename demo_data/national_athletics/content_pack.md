## 6. Detailed Performance Review

### 6.1 Mei Xin — Long Jump U12 Girls

Mei Xin delivered an outstanding performance in the Long Jump U12 Girls category. She showed strong focus during the qualification round and maintained excellent composure in the final. Her winning performance secured the gold medal and established a new national primary schools record.

Her performance reflected several strengths:

- consistent run-up rhythm;
- strong take-off confidence;
- ability to adjust technique after feedback;
- emotional stability under national-level pressure;
- high level of self-discipline during preparation.

As a result of this achievement, Mei Xin has been selected for the **Malaysia Schools Invitational Athletics Meet in Singapore**. Further preparation will be required, including training schedule confirmation, parent consent, travel documentation, and coordination with the relevant sports authorities.

### 6.2 Xiao Le — 100m U12 Boys

Xiao Le represented the school in the 100m U12 Boys event at national level. He did not win a medal this time, and his final time was below his personal best. Training attendance and consistency need to be strengthened; with more regular training his acceleration, running posture, and finishing effort can improve.

His strengths included:

- natural sprint speed;
- strong acceleration in the first phase of the race;
- competitive spirit;
- improved confidence in the final heat.

However, his training consistency should continue to be strengthened. Maintaining regular attendance at scheduled training sessions and improving finishing discipline will be important for his future development. This recommendation is based solely on training records and student development needs. It is not influenced by family background, parent status, PIBG role, social title, or household income.

### 6.3 Ali — Shot Put U12 Boys

Ali achieved a silver medal in the Shot Put U12 Boys category. Compared with his earlier qualification performance, he demonstrated clear improvement in body coordination, release angle, and confidence during competition.

His performance showed:

- improved throwing technique;
- better stability during release;
- willingness to accept coaching feedback;
- stronger mental readiness during competition.

Further focus should be placed on strength conditioning, safe technique reinforcement, and consistency across all attempts.

   Pupils should experience more simulated competition environments before major events.

5. **Documentation preparation**
